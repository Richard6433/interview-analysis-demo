# Interview analysis platform: demo

A click-through demo of a local platform for qualitative interview analysis, built during a master's
internship (University of Turin, ICT4D) with the Civic Literacy Initiative.

**Open the demo:** https://richard6433.github.io/interview-analysis-demo/

- The three interviews are **invented**: no real person, organisation or interview.
- The AI agents are **simulated**: each step finishes in a few seconds and replays prepared results.
- Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. "Start the demo again" resets it.

In the real platform, each step is run by the researcher's own Claude subscription on their own
computer, and the interview data never leaves it.
